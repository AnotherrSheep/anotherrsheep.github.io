Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24th August, 2026

This web page represents the privacy policy of the City University of Hong Kong Choir website. As we are NOT the administrator of the domain www.cityu.edu.hk, this privacy notice will only be applied this this site, referring to pages under the domain www.cityu.edu.hk/choir/. By using this website, you sgnify your acceptance and acknowledgement of this privacy notice. If you do not agree to any part of the notice, you must immediately discontinue access to our Website.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information from you in the following ways:

  1. Information You Provide Directly: We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. This includes, but is not limited to:
    • Contact Information: When you fill out our application via Google Forms or contact us through our website, we may collect your name, email address, and phone number.
    • Participation Details: When applying for the choir, you may provide information regarding your musical experience and availability.
  2. Information Collected via Third-Party Services: Our website links to social media platforms (e.g., Instagram, Facebook). We do not collect data from these platforms; however, these platforms may have their own data-gathering practices. Furthermore, we utilize Google Forms for applications; your data provided there is managed in accordance with Google's privacy standards and our internal internal records.
  3. Automatically Collected Information: Like most websites, we may automatically collect certain information (such as your IP address, browser type, and visit duration) through cookies or similar tracking technologies to help us improve the user experience and understand how visitors interact with our site.

Cookies

Our website uses basic cookies to ensure it works correctly. Additionally, our site contains links to social media platforms. Please note that once you click on these links, you are subject to the privacy policies of those external platforms (e.g., Instagram, Facebook).

Third-Party Services

Third-Party Services Our website utilizes third-party services to enhance functionality and provide a better user experience. These include, but are not limited to:

  • Application Processing: We use Google Forms to collect and manage applications for City University of Hong Kong Choir. Any information submitted through this form is used solely for the purpose of processing your application and facilitating communication regarding choir activities.
  • Social Media Integration: Our website contains links to social media platforms (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, X). By clicking these links, you are directed to those platforms. These platforms are governed by their own terms of service and privacy policies. City University of Hong Kong Choir is not responsible for any data collection or practices performed by these third-party platforms.

Privacy Policy Changes

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Notice. If we make a material change to this Privacy Notice, we will notify you by posting the change on this website and in this Privacy Notice and, if necessary, give you additional choices regarding such change prior to the material change becoming effective.

If you feel that this site is not following its stated information policy, please contact us via email: a-choir@um.cityu.edu.hk
